# Orbis Arena

A lane battler minigame for Hytale servers. Type `/arena`, pick a faction in the lobby, and when the countdown
runs out you face another player (or the bot) from above: play cards from your hand with essence, send Kweebecs,
Trorks, the Undead or Goblins over the bridges, build walls, hedges and bridges out of real blocks, and bring down
the enemy keep while your avatar defends yours.

- 4 factions with 8 cards each, an ability for every avatar
- 4 arenas after the zones of Orbis
- 3 minutes, double essence in the last minute, sudden death after a tie

`/arena leave` takes you back to where you came from, with your inventory.
